Why I Teach Hebrew

From Tel Aviv to Zurich – the story behind my teaching

I grew up in Tel Aviv and studied Education and Jewish Studies. That's where my love for Hebrew deepened – a language that carries thousands of years of history and culture.

When I moved to Zurich, I discovered something moving: here, far from Israel, people were searching for exactly what I had always taken for granted – the connection to the language, the culture, the tradition.

That's how teaching Hebrew became my passion.

Who learns Hebrew with me

Every student comes with a different background, but they all share the desire for connection.

There's the business professional who wants to learn Hebrew for work and discovers it goes far beyond meetings – it's about understanding colleagues, their humour, the way Israelis think.

There's the person looking for roots and connection – a Jewish grandmother, perhaps family in Israel, or a bond that was lost over time.

And there's the culture enthusiast – someone who fell in love with Israel and wants to read newspapers, understand songs, truly experience it.

Why I do this

There's a magical moment in teaching – when the eyes light up and the student says: "Now I get it!"

They read a sentence on their own and understand it. They start to speak – with mistakes, yes, and words still missing – but they speak.

That's the moment they feel: all the effort, the time invested – it was worth it. Hebrew feels less foreign. They're communicating. It actually works.

And those moments, when I see their joy – it's like a gardener looking at the garden they planted and nurtured. That's when I know: a new door has opened for them.
